Our story

Built to connect Canadians
on the road

Portail was created to solve a simple problem: getting from city to city in Canada is expensive and often inconvenient. We built a platform where drivers with empty seats can share their journey — and passengers can travel affordably.

Timeline

How we got here

2024

The idea

Portail began as a concept to make intercity travel more affordable for Canadians — especially for routes not well served by public transit.

Late 2025

Building the app

Development started on the iOS and Android apps, along with the admin platform. Months of building, testing, and refining the booking, payment, and driver verification flows.

June 2026

Launch

Portail officially launches on the App Store and Google Play — bringing affordable carpooling to passengers and a new income stream to drivers across Canada.
