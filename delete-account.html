Account management

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Portail account at any time directly from the app. Your request is processed within 24 hours.

How to delete

Step-by-step instructions

1

Open the Portail app

Make sure you are signed in to the account you want to delete.

2

Go to Settings

Tap your profile icon, then tap Settings at the bottom of the screen.

3

Tap "Delete Account"

Scroll to the bottom of Settings and tap Delete Account.

4

Confirm your request

Type DELETE in the confirmation field, then tap the delete button. Your request is sent to our team immediately.

5

Account removed within 24 hours

Our team processes deletion requests promptly. Your account and personal data will be fully removed within 24 hours of your request.

What gets deleted

  • Name, email, and phone number
  • Profile photo
  • Vehicle information
  • Trip history and bookings
  • Saved payment methods
  • Ratings and reviews

What may be retained

  • Transaction records for tax and legal compliance (up to 7 years, as required by Canadian law)
  • Anonymized and aggregated platform data
⚠️ This action is permanent. Once deleted, your account and data cannot be recovered. Please cancel any active bookings before submitting a deletion request.

Don't have access to the app? Email us at hello@portailapp.ca to request deletion.
