How it works

Simple from start to finish

01

Create your account

Sign up as a passenger looking for rides, or as a driver ready to share your trip and earn money.

02

Find or post a trip

Passengers search by destination and date. Drivers post trips with pickup points, pricing, and seat availability.

03

Book & travel

Book your seat instantly or request approval from the driver. Pay securely in-app, and hit the road.

04

Earn as a driver

Set your price per seat, accept passengers, and receive payouts directly to your bank account.
